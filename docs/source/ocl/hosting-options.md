## Hosting Options

*This page is a stub. It lists the ways OCL can be run today; each option still needs a full write-up.*

OCL is open source under MPL-2.0. You can use the hosted service, have a deployment run for you, or run it entirely on your own infrastructure — and move between these as your needs change. The terminology content and the APIs are the same in every case.

### OCL Online (hosted by OCL)

The hosted service at [app.openconceptlab.org](https://app.openconceptlab.org). Browsing, searching and publishing public terminology is free; paid plans add private repositories, the OCL Mapper and higher limits. Nothing to install — upgrades, backups and monitoring are handled for you.

Best for: most individuals and teams, and anyone who wants to start today.

### Managed deployment (run for you by OCL)

A dedicated deployment operated by the OCL team — your own instance, sized to your workload, with uptime commitments, a formal SLA and named support contacts. Suited to national deployments and to organizations integrating OCL transactionally with their own systems (EMR, data warehouse, HIE).

Best for: production and mission-critical use where you want a dedicated instance without running it yourself.

Contact us at info@openconceptlab.org to discuss a managed deployment.

### Self-hosting (run by you)

Run OCL on your own servers, in your own cloud account, or inside an air-gapped network. There is no license fee. You are responsible for deployment, upgrades, backups and monitoring.

See [Self-hosting OCL](self-hosting.md) for configuration notes.

Best for: organizations with data-residency or network constraints, and those with the operational capacity to run it.

### Choosing between them

| | OCL Online | Managed deployment | Self-hosted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Who operates it | OCL | OCL | You |
| Dedicated instance | No | Yes | Yes |
| Uptime guarantee / SLA | — | Yes | Your own |
| Runs inside your network | No | By arrangement | Yes |
| Cost | Free tier plus paid plans | Custom | Infrastructure only |
